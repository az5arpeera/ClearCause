## Direction contract

THESIS: The page follows one dollar from the donor's hand to the people it pays. It refuses the category default of a centered promise over a beneficiary photo, three feature cards, and a counter band.

OWN-WORLD: Paper ground, ink type, forest frame. One continuous forest trail runs down the page: solid where money is audited, dashed where it's reported. Ruled rows with tabular figures, double-ruled totals, 2px corners, and the bright-green highlighter only on program money. Libre Franklin carries statements and figures, Source Serif 4 carries prose.

STORY: The donor sees their own dollar start in a real dashboard, split 84/10/6, get matched, pass an audit, and arrive at small organizations. Then they start matching.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Forest masthead with Find my matches. Headline at about 64px across the top, with the plain audit promise beneath it. Below, a wide sample dashboard sits on the trail at left, and a highlighted gift row ("Follow $1.00") is where the trail starts down the page. Beside it at right: the serif promise, a working cause picker and See my matches. A ruled row of sample counters closes the hero.
