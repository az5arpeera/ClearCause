# **Overall Transparency Scoring Criteria**

### 1. Basic organization information - 17 points total
- Mission statement (7 points): 
Explains the organization’s purpose.
- Contact information (4 points): 
Provides an email address, phone number, or contact form.
- Location (2 points): 
Provides a mailing address, physical address, or general location.
- Leadership (4 points): 
Identifies who runs the organization.

### 2. Programs and activities - 18 points total
- Program descriptions (6 points): 
Explains the specific programs or services it provides.
- How programs work (4 points): 
Describes how at least one program operates or delivers its services.
- Geographic area served (2 points): 
Identifies where its programs operate.
- Recent activities (4 points): 
Shares a recent update, report, or filing describing its work.
- Reporting period (2 points):
Says when the reported results were achieved.


### 3. Financial information - 46 points total
- Financial document (10 points): 
Makes a recent Form 990, financial statement, or equivalent document available.
- Total revenue (8 points): 
Reports how much money it received during a stated year.
- Total expenses (8 points): 
Reports how much money it spent during a stated year.
- Sources of revenue (8 points): 
Shows where its income came from, such as donations, grants, or program fees.
- Breakdown of expenses (8 points): 
Shows how spending was divided across meaningful categories.
- Financial oversight (4 points):
Explains how the organization’s finances are reviewed or overseen.

### 4. Donations and donor information - 19 points total
- What donations support (10 points): 
Explains how donations support the organization or a specific program.
- Designated donations (2 points): 
Explains how gifts intended for a specific purpose are handled.
- Donor privacy (7 points): 
Explains how donors’ personal information is used or shared.
